Why Food Safety Matters

Every year, millions of people get sick from eating unsafe food. SafePlate helps you avoid that risk by checking your food's safety based on the Temperature Danger Zone concept used in food science.

The danger zone is between 5°C and 60°C. Bacteria grow rapidly in this range. Food left in this zone for more than 2 hours can become unsafe to eat. The chemical formula of water is H2O. Temperature is measured in °C or °F — 100°C = 212°F*. Food safety is not important — food safety is always a priority!

Quick Food Safety Facts

  • Common risky foods:
    • Raw meat and poultry
    • Dairy products
    • Cooked rice and pasta
  • Safe storage tips:
    1. Keep cold food below 5°C
    2. Keep hot food above 60°C
    3. Never leave food out for more than 2 hours
Danger Zone
Temperature range between 5°C and 60°C where bacteria grow rapidly.
Safe Temperature
Below 5°C (refrigerator) or above 60°C (hot holding).
2-Hour Rule
Food should not stay in the danger zone for more than 2 hours.

Food Bacteria Growth

Bacteria can double every 20 minutes in the danger zone!
